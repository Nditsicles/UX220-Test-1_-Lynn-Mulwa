Tim Berners-Lee

Timothy (Tim) Berners-Lee is an English Computer scientist best known for inventing the World Wide Web in 1989 while working at CERN, a European Particle Physics Laboratory. He also invented HTML, the URL system and HTTP. In 1994 he founded the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) to guide the web's long-term development, and he has been a strong advocate for keeping it open and free for everyone. HE is currently working as a research fellow at the University of Oxford and a professor emeritus at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)
